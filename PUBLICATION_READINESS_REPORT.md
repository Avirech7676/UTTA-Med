## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

This report represents the definitive, independent scientific evaluation of the UTTA-Med 2.0 project. As requested by the research team and committee, every major experimental claim has been audited without adjusting numbers to match prior narratives.

### Core Scientific Verdict:
1. **Does UTTA-Med 2.0 genuinely outperform Confidence Gating?**  
   **NO.** In the authentic empirical data across 85,054 histology patches and 5 random seeds, **Confidence Gating is strictly superior to Original UTTA-Med** (Binary F1 $+0.0013$, $p = 0.0062$; Macro F1 $+0.0010$, $p = 0.0050$; AUROC $+0.0005$, $p = 0.0161$; Harm reduction $-0.027\%$, $p = 0.0155$). Claims that UTTA-Med 2.0 (A9) achieved higher F1 ($0.8342$) than Confidence Gating ($0.8291$) were generated by a synthetic CPU worker injecting random normal jitter into hardcoded prior constants (`run_kaggle.py`).
2. **Does Bio-SafeAdapt genuinely improve mitotic figure detection?**  
   **ALGORITHMICALLY SOUND, BUT EMPIRICALLY UNVERIFIED.** The optical density cellularity gating algorithm is cleanly implemented and logically sound in code (`src/v2/safeadapt/bio_safeadapt.py`). However, the reported improvement from $F_1 = 0.4774$ to $0.4882$ was hardcoded in `run_kaggle.py` without evaluating real MIDOG target whole-slide images. The number `0.4774` was a train-validation score from Seed 42, not a target test baseline.
3. **What is the true, authentic contribution of this research?**  
   The true, scientifically verified contribution is an **empirical reliability and failure-mode study of test-time adaptation in digital pathology**:
   - Demonstrating that unconstrained entropy minimization (Tent) collapses catastrophically on histology whole-slide images (AUROC drops to $0.34$, harm $>30\%$).
   - Proving that complex 20-pass Monte Carlo Dropout is a costly computational bottleneck ($20\times$ slowdown) that underperforms simple, deterministic single-pass confidence gating.
   - Proving that the SafeAdapt rollback safeguard successfully prevents catastrophic drift on real whole-slide images, safely falling back to the certified source model when severe domain shifts occur.

## 3. Publication Demarcation & Manuscript Action Plan

To ensure that the IEEE manuscript adheres to the highest standards of scientific integrity, the research team must implement the following three editorial actions:

### Action 1: Retract the Synthetic V2 Sweep
* **What to Remove:** Delete all references to the synthetic 50-arm table in `UTTA_MED_V2_RESULTS.csv`, the claimed $p = 0.0961$ F1 improvement, and the synthetic $F_1 = 0.4882$ Bio-SafeAdapt score.
* **Why:** Submitting synthetic numbers generated via `np.random.normal` to an IEEE journal risks immediate desk rejection and severe institutional reputational damage.

### Action 2: Lead with the Verified Empirical Contrast (Confidence vs. UTTA-Med)
* **What to Highlight:** Position the empirical discovery that **Confidence Gating outperforms 20-pass Monte Carlo Dropout** as the central scientific insight of the paper:
  > *"Across 85,054 whole-slide patches evaluated across five distinct training seeds on Camelyon17-WILDS Center 2, single-pass confidence gating ($\tau = 0.90$) consistently outperformed 20-pass Monte Carlo Dropout predictive variance gating across all five seeds ($p = 0.0062$, paired Cohen's $d_z = 2.36$). Confidence gating achieved superior F1, higher AUROC, lower harmful adaptation, and a $20\times$ reduction in forward pass compute. This demonstrates that multi-pass epistemic variance is an unjustified computational bottleneck in clinical whole-slide streaming."*
* **Impact:** Reviewers will praise this transparent, counter-intuitive empirical finding because it challenges prevailing assumptions in test-time adaptation literature.

### Action 3: Present SafeAdapt and Adaptive Gating as Safeguard Frameworks
* **Framing:**
  - Frame **SafeAdapt** as an indispensable clinical safeguard that successfully prevented catastrophic collapse on MIDOG Scanner 3 by reverting to the source model when adaptation drifted.
  - Frame **Adaptive Gating** as an inference optimization framework that reduces MC Dropout latency by $10\times$ for settings where stochastic uncertainty is mandated by institutional governance.
  - Frame **Bio-SafeAdapt** as an architectural proposal for biological context-conditioning whose full clinical whole-slide validation is documented as promising future work.
