## What now replicates on 3/3 seeds

1. Cross-hospital shift is real (AUROC drop 0.064–0.067; F1 drop 0.15–0.19).
2. MC-Dropout ranks errors (r ≈ 0.40).
3. **Gating** (confidence or UTTA) beats source on AUROC and F1; harm is ~10× lower than Tent.
4. **UTTA-Med ≈ confidence** (Δ AUROC < 0.001). Entropy ≡ confidence in binary classification.
5. Random is not a substitute: seed 2024 random **collapses** (AUROC 0.897, harm 0.104). The win is *which* samples, not fewer samples.
6. Fair Tent does **not** reliably recover shift (mean AUROC ≈ source; F1 often worse; ECE worse).

Tent AUROC sign is unstable across seeds. Do not headline Tent. Headline **selective TTA**, and the honest null of MC-Dropout vs confidence.
